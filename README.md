## Current Research

My current work focuses on analytical and numerical methods for open quantum systems, including:

* GKSL and Redfield master equations
* Influence functional techniques for bosonic and fermionic baths
* TEMPO and HEOM methods
* Quantum transport and thermodynamics
* Volterra integral equation approaches

## Featured Repositories

### Open Quantum Systems

Numerical and analytical studies of non-Markovian quantum dynamics, TEMPO, HEOM, and benchmark calculations.

### Active Particle Simulations

Fortran and Python implementations of Run-and-Tumble particles, Active Brownian Particles, and Vicsek-type models.

### Monte Carlo Methods

Classical Monte Carlo algorithms including random walks, sampling methods, and Ising-model simulations.
